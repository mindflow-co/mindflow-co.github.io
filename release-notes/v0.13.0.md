# Mindflow v0.13.0

**Released:** 2026-08-25

## What's new

- **Mindflow tells you when your microphone isn't sending anything.** A microphone can be connected, selected, and reporting itself as perfectly healthy while delivering no audio at all — a Bluetooth headset whose voice link never came up does exactly this. Nothing errors, so until now dictation simply did nothing and a meeting recorded only the other participants. Mindflow now notices within a couple of seconds, names the device, and tells you where to change it.

- **Copy your last dictation straight from the menu bar.** Dictation usually types into whatever field you're in, but when it can't — no Accessibility permission, or focus moved — the text ended up in a list behind Settings. There's now a copy button in the panel header.

## Fixes

- **Recogniser markup no longer leaks into transcripts.** The Arabic model marks non-speech moments inline and escapes ampersands; those tags occasionally appeared mid-sentence in finished transcripts. They're now removed before the transcript is written.

## Under the hood

- The microphone warning during meetings previously existed only in a debug log that macOS discards, so nobody ever saw it. It now reaches the transcript's own event log and the app.

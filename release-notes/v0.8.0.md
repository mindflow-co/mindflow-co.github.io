# Mindflow v0.8.0

**Released:** 2026-08-20

## Read this first — you will be asked for permission once

Mindflow no longer asks for **Screen Recording**. It now asks only for **System Audio Recording**,
a narrower macOS permission that grants access to sound and nothing else. Mindflow never looked at
your screen, and now it cannot.

Because macOS treats these as two different permissions, the old grant does not carry over: the
first time you record after updating, macOS will ask once. Click Allow and you are done. If you
want to tidy up, you can remove Mindflow from **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen &
System Audio Recording** — it belongs in the **System Audio Recording Only** list underneath now.

This release also requires **macOS 14.2 or later** (previously 14.0), because the audio API it uses
was introduced in 14.2.

## What's new

- Meetings you forget about now stop on their own. If a recording hears nothing for 15 minutes,
  Mindflow ends it and saves the transcript. You can change the timeout or switch it off in
  Settings → Transcription.
- The menu bar icon now shows what Mindflow is doing. It turns into a red capsule with a record dot
  while a meeting is being captured, and goes amber when the room has gone quiet — so a running
  meeting is obvious at a glance instead of something you have to remember.
- Meeting auto-detection works in every mainstream browser. Previously only Chrome and Brave were
  recognised, so a Google Meet call in Edge, Safari, Firefox, Arc, Vivaldi or Opera was invisible.
  All of them are detected now.
- Settings → Models now tells you which engine actually transcribes your meetings. English meetings
  run on Parakeet; the whisper model you select is the fallback used at start-up, on errors, for
  other languages, and for translation. The old screen implied whisper did the work.
- Transcripts record which engine produced each line, so you can tell at a glance whether a passage
  came from Parakeet, whisper, or Arabic Pro.

## Fixes

- A meeting could silently stop capturing the other participants and carry on as if nothing was
  wrong. Unplugging headphones mid-call was enough to trigger it — the rest of the meeting recorded
  your microphone only. Mindflow now notices, reconnects automatically, and tells you if it cannot.
- Recording a single app by name captured silence for most apps. Apps like Slack, Edge and Chrome
  play audio from helper processes, and only the main one was being listened to.
- In a room with background noise, a meeting could run for hours without ever being recognised as
  silent — which meant the new auto-stop would never have fired for exactly the people who need it.
- The Home window claimed "No model installed" for anyone whose selected model was no longer in the
  catalogue, even with other models installed and recording working normally.
- "Re-check Permissions" reported success without actually being able to check anything, so a stale
  warning survived after you had fixed the permission.
- A partly-downloaded Arabic Pro model could be shown as active with a Delete button that destroyed
  the download's resume state.
- The app redrew its entire interface every five seconds while sitting completely idle.

## Under the hood

- System audio is now captured with Core Audio process taps instead of ScreenCaptureKit. This is
  what makes the narrower permission possible, and it removes the dummy video stream Mindflow
  previously had to run.
- **English (Standard)** has been removed from the model list. At 1.5 GB it was both larger and less
  accurate than **Universal (Fast)** at 1.6 GB, which also handles every other language. If you had
  it downloaded it still works and can still be deleted from Settings → Models.
- The automated test suite grew from 35 to 86 tests, mostly covering the new audio path.

# Mindflow v0.10.3

**Released:** 2026-08-23

## Fixes

- **One person is no longer split across several speaker names.** A call with two people could come
  out with three or four different speakers in the transcript, with the other participant switching
  between names mid-conversation. Two separate causes, both fixed:

  Speaker identification only manages to label some of what it hears, and everything it skipped was
  being shown under a generic "Remote" heading — so the gaps in its work looked like an extra person
  in the room. Those stretches are now attributed to whoever was speaking around them.

  Separately, speaker identification listens to everyone on the far end through a single combined
  audio stream, and it sometimes divides one voice in two when the sound shifts — a codec change,
  someone moving away from their microphone. Mindflow now expects a genuine second participant to
  carry a real share of the conversation before treating them as separate. When it is unsure it
  keeps people together, because one person appearing under two names is far more confusing to read
  than two quiet people sharing one.

  Calls where several people genuinely speak from the far end are unaffected and still get
  individual speakers.

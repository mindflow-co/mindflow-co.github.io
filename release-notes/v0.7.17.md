# Mindflow v0.7.17

**Released:** 2026-08-19

## Fixes

- **Unfinished recordings are no longer deleted on the next launch.** When a meeting stops, Mindflow moves its audio aside to work out who was speaking. If the app closed before that finished — a crash, a forced quit, a machine restart — the next launch was deleting that audio, and no transcript had been written yet. It is the only copy at that moment, so this could lose a recording outright, and it was worst for long meetings where the speaker pass takes longest. Mindflow now recognises unfinished recordings on launch and simply finishes them. If it cannot work out which meeting some leftover audio belongs to, it keeps the audio rather than deleting it, and says so in its logs.

## Under the hood

- Recordings staged for speaker identification now carry a small manifest naming the meeting they belong to, which is what makes the recovery above possible — previously that link existed only in memory and was lost the moment the app closed.
- Arabic transcription now records a per-segment confidence figure and a note of whether the anti-repetition guard fired. Nothing changes in what you see; it means that if a transcript ever comes out wrong, there is now a record of what the engine thought it was doing, instead of having to reproduce the problem to investigate it.

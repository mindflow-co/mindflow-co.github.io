# Mindflow v0.12.1

**Released:** 2026-08-25

## Fixes

- **One side of a meeting could quietly stop being transcribed.** On a long call, either your microphone or the other participants could drop out of the transcript partway through and never come back — while the recording itself carried on perfectly. The audio was always saved; it just stopped being turned into text, and nothing said so. Seen on a 58-minute call where the last 38 minutes of one speaker went missing from the transcript despite the recording being flawless.

- **If it happens again, Mindflow now tells you.** A stalled stream raises a warning during the meeting instead of failing silently, and says plainly that the recording is safe — because it is. Mindflow also restarts that part of the pipeline on its own, so the stream recovers rather than staying dead for the rest of the call.

## Under the hood

- Transcription no longer trusts a plain "busy" flag that could be left set forever by a call that never returned. A run that overruns its allowance is superseded, and a late-returning run can't disturb the one that replaced it.
- Audio is now tracked both entering and leaving the conversion step, which is the only way to tell "this person is receiving audio that never gets transcribed" apart from "this person stopped talking".
- Warnings from the capture path are written where they survive the meeting. The earlier outage left nothing behind to diagnose, because that code logged at a level macOS discards.

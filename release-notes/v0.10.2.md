# Mindflow v0.10.2

**Released:** 2026-08-21

## Fixes

- **The same person is no longer split across two names in a transcript.** A two-person call could
  come out with three speaker labels — you, plus the other person appearing sometimes as "Remote"
  and sometimes as "Speaker 1", switching back and forth mid-conversation. Speaker identification
  had been allowed to override the far more reliable signal of which audio channel the speech
  arrived on. It is now only used when it has actually found two or more distinct voices to tell
  apart, which is the only situation where those labels mean anything. Calls with several people on
  the far side are unaffected and still get individual speaker labels.

- **Your own speech is never relabelled.** Your microphone has exactly one person on it, so a
  display name you have set is now always used for it.

- **Recordings stay in step with the clock on every audio device.** Following yesterday's fix for
  distorted remote audio, Mindflow now re-checks the true recording rate whenever your audio device
  changes mode — which Bluetooth headsets do the moment a call connects — instead of only once at
  the start. It also recognises a wider range of professional audio hardware.

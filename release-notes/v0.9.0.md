# Mindflow v0.9.0

**Released:** 2026-08-20

## Pause a meeting without ending it

You can now pause a recording. Capture stops immediately — nothing is transcribed, and nothing
reaches the saved audio — while the meeting stays open and everything captured so far is kept
exactly as it was. Press Resume and it picks up where it left off.

Use it for the moment someone says "can we go off the record", for a side conversation, or for the
break in the middle of a long call.

- **From the menu bar:** open Mindflow while recording and press **Pause**. The button becomes
  **Resume**.
- **From Claude Code:** `control_meeting` now takes `pause` and `resume` alongside `start` and
  `stop`.
- **The menu bar icon tells you which kind of pause you are in.** Grey means you paused it and it
  will stay paused until you say otherwise. Amber still means Mindflow went quiet on its own after
  30 seconds of silence and will resume itself at the next sound. Those are genuinely different
  situations and now they look different.

A few details worth knowing:

- **Paused time is preserved in the transcript rather than hidden.** Timestamps continue to match
  the real clock, so a meeting where you paused for ten minutes shows a ten-minute gap rather than
  quietly stitching the two halves together. What was said before and after stays where it actually
  happened.
- **A paused meeting is never auto-stopped.** The 15-minute silence auto-stop added in v0.8.0 exists
  to catch meetings you forgot to end. While paused nothing is being recorded, so there is nothing
  to catch — and stopping would throw away your intention to come back. Pause lasts as long as you
  want it to.
- **Pausing does not disturb the recording in any way.** Stop still works normally from a paused
  meeting, and the transcript saves exactly as it would have.

## Under the hood

- The automated test suite grew to 138 tests, 12 of them covering pause specifically — including
  that talking during a pause cannot un-pause it, and that resuming after a long pause does not
  inherit the silence that accumulated while you were away.

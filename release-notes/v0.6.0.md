# Mindflow v0.6.0

**Released:** 2026-06-26

## What's new
- **Audio recordings, saved locally.** Mindflow now keeps a playback recording of each meeting next to its transcript — your microphone and the system audio mixed into a single track, captured and stored entirely on your Mac.
- **A built-in player on the transcript screen.** Open any meeting (in the Past Meetings window or the post-meeting window) and you'll see a player at the top with play/pause, a draggable scrubber, and skip-back / skip-forward 10-second buttons.
- **Rename, move, and label from the transcript itself.** You can now rename a meeting, move it to a folder, and add or remove labels right from the transcript screen — no need to go back to the meetings list. Removing a label is now possible too.
- **A cleaner meeting header.** Every transcript now opens with a tidy header showing the title, folder, labels, duration, and start/end times.
- **A "Save audio recordings" toggle** in Settings (on by default). Recordings are about 14 MB per hour and are saved at voice quality (16 kHz mono).

## Fixes
- Transcripts no longer show a block of raw metadata text (`display_name`, `labels`, `folder`, …) at the top — that information now lives in the new header instead.

## Under the hood
- Recordings are stored as a compact `.m4a` alongside each transcript and travel with the meeting when you move it or remove it (including via the AI-assistant tools).
- No new permissions are required — Mindflow already used your microphone and system-audio access for live transcription; this feature simply saves that audio. Everything stays on-device.

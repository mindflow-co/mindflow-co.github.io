# Mindflow v0.12.2

**Released:** 2026-08-25

## What's new

- **Mindflow now warns you when your microphone is too quiet.** Transcription is tuned for a certain input level, and below it accuracy degrades quietly — words get dropped, sentences get cut short — with nothing to indicate the cause is gain rather than the recogniser. If your microphone is consistently coming through too softly, Mindflow now says so once during the meeting and tells you where to fix it. Your audio is still recorded either way.

## Under the hood

- The warning is deliberately conservative: it needs several minutes of actual speech before it will claim anything, silence never counts as evidence, and natural variation in how loudly you speak will not trigger it.

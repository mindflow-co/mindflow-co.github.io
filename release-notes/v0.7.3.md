# Mindflow v0.7.3

**Released:** 2026-07-10

Emergency fix: Mindflow could crash a few seconds after launch. Everything from v0.7.2 is included — if you're on v0.7.0, v0.7.1, or v0.7.2, update now.

## Fixes
- Fixed a crash that could kill Mindflow about five seconds after launch (when the Home window drew the dictation shortcut). The app package was missing components that the keyboard-shortcut system needs; the flaw affected v0.7.0 through v0.7.2 but was masked on our development machine, which is why it escaped testing. If Mindflow ever "quit unexpectedly" right after opening, this release is the cure.

## What's new
- Everything from v0.7.2 ships in this build: ask-before-recording with meeting detection that only reacts to apps actively using your microphone, the free 30-day trial and offline licensing, and speaker-identification progress on the meeting screen. See the v0.7.2 notes for details.

## Under the hood
- The app packaging step now bundles every component library's resources and refuses to build a package with any of them missing, and our release checks now include launching the app on a simulated "clean machine" so this class of bug can't slip through again.

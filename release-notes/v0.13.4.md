# Mindflow v0.13.4

**Released:** 2026-09-08

## What's new

- Nothing new this release — it is entirely fixes.

## Fixes

- Ending a meeting no longer shows a "Transcription engine crashed" alert a few seconds after you have already stopped. The alert was alarming but the real cost was quieter: the last few seconds of a meeting could be dropped from the transcript. Mindflow now waits for every piece of audio it has already picked up to finish transcribing before it cleans up, so the tail of your meeting is transcribed and saved like the rest of it.
- When the transcription engine does fail, the alert now names the engine that actually failed. Arabic meetings run on a different engine from English ones, and the alert always claimed it was the English one — which sent anyone trying to debug it after the wrong thing.
- Pressing Escape during dictation no longer throws away what you just said. Escape now means "do not paste this", not "forget it happened": the text is still transcribed and still saved to your dictation History, so you can copy it back if you hit the key by accident.
- Dictation now recovers when a Bluetooth headset's microphone never connects. Some headsets — a Sony WH-1000XM6 was the reported case — can be connected for audio while the microphone link is held by another device, such as your phone. Mindflow used to hear silence and give up. It now waits long enough for the headset to hand the microphone over, and falls back to your built-in microphone if it never does.

## Under the hood

- The shutdown path that drops queued audio is covered by a new entry in the project's internal notes, along with the reasoning behind the bounded wait that keeps a slow or wedged transcription from hanging the end of a meeting.

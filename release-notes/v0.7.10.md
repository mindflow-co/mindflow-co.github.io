# Mindflow v0.7.10

**Released:** 2026-07-20

A polish release: the Models settings screen becomes self-contained with its own language picker, the menu bar popover gets a cleaner layout, and a rare remote-control reliability hole is closed.

## What's new
- **Language picker in Settings → Models.** The Models screen always filtered its list to models that support your transcription language, but changing the language meant a detour to another tab. Now the picker sits right above the model list — pick a language and the list updates instantly. It stays in sync with the pickers in the menu bar and General settings, and locks during an active recording (switching languages mid-meeting would garble the transcript).

## Fixes
- The quick-action buttons at the bottom of the menu bar popover no longer squeeze into one crowded row — labels like "Meetings (59)" used to wrap onto two lines. They're now arranged in two tidy rows with room to breathe.
- On first launch, the Mindflow menu bar icon now appears near the system icons on the right side of the menu bar instead of getting buried at the far left of a crowded bar. If you've ever ⌘-dragged the icon to a spot you prefer, Mindflow respects your placement and never moves it.
- The Quality tab in Settings → Models had misaligned headers and controls scattered across the pane. It now uses the same clean grouped layout as every other settings screen.
- Closed a rare edge case where a stale leftover control command (from AI-assistant integrations like Claude Code) could trigger an unintended recording start or stop. Commands are now consumed the moment they're handled and any stale command is discarded at launch, so a recording can only ever start from a fresh, deliberate request.

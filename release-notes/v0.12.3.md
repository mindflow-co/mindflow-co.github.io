# Mindflow v0.12.3

**Released:** 2026-08-25

## Fixes

- **"System Audio Recording is off" could get stuck on after you fixed it.** macOS gives apps no way to ask whether this permission has been granted, so Mindflow works it out from what it hears and remembers the answer. Once it concluded the permission was off, the warning's "Fix" button only opened System Settings — where the toggle was often already switched on, leaving nowhere to go. "Fix" now also clears the remembered answer, so the warning goes away and Mindflow re-checks on your next recording.

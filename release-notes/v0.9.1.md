# Mindflow v0.9.1

**Released:** 2026-08-20

## Fixes

- **The menu bar icon no longer changes colour on its own while you record.** It could turn from red
  to orange partway through a meeting that was recording perfectly well, and back again — because
  Mindflow was showing you an internal "gone quiet for 30 seconds" state as though recording had
  paused. It had not: audio kept being captured and transcribed the whole time. Since ordinary
  conversations have pauses in them, the icon ended up changing colour repeatedly during a single
  meeting for no reason you could act on.

  The icon now shows only the three things that actually differ: not recording, recording, and
  paused by you.

- **Paused now looks distinctly different from recording.** Red means Mindflow is capturing. Amber
  with pause bars means you pressed Pause and it will stay that way until you press Resume.

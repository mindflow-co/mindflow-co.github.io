# Mindflow v0.11.0

**Released:** 2026-08-23

## Jump straight to any moment in a recording

Every timestamp in a transcript is now a play button. Click one and the recording jumps to that
line and starts playing — no more dragging the scrubber trying to find the bit you were reading.

The button only appears where Mindflow can be certain which moment a line refers to. If a transcript
was saved without timestamps, or the timestamp cannot be matched to a point in the audio, the time
is shown as plain text rather than a button that would take you somewhere wrong.

## Re-render old transcripts to pick up improvements

A transcript on disk is a rendering of the underlying recording log, so improvements to speaker
labelling or formatting only ever reached *new* recordings. Anything already saved kept whatever
rules were in force the day it was made.

You can now rebuild them:

- **One meeting** — the circular-arrows button next to Copy and Export in the transcript view.
- **Several at once** — select meetings in the list and use **Re-render** in the selection bar.
- **From Claude Code** — the new `rerender_transcripts` tool, with or without a specific meeting.

This is worth doing after v0.10.3, which stopped a single person being split across several speaker
names. Re-rendering applies that fix to meetings you already have.

**Nothing is re-transcribed and no words change.** Only speaker labels, paragraph grouping and
timestamp formatting are rebuilt, from the original recording log. Meetings saved without that log —
older or imported ones — are skipped rather than altered.

Verified across a full archive of 45 transcripts before release: zero words lost or added in any of
them.

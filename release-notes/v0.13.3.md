# Mindflow v0.13.3

**Released:** 2026-08-26

## Fixes

- **Dictation no longer fails on the first try.** If you use a Bluetooth headset as your microphone, macOS often delivers no audio at all for the first few seconds after you start recording — so the first dictation caught nothing and you had to trigger it a second or third time. Mindflow now rebuilds the audio connection automatically while you keep talking, instead of waiting on a device that isn't going to answer. In testing, every session that previously failed now recovers.

- **You'll be told when nothing was heard.** Previously a lost sentence and an accidental hotkey press looked exactly the same: the window just closed. If dictation captures no audio at all, Mindflow now says so clearly and offers to open your Sound settings, so you know to try again instead of discovering later that your words never arrived. A brief accidental press is still ignored quietly.

## Under the hood

- The previous release gave up after 2.5 seconds of silence. That was too aggressive for a device that takes longer to wake, and it turned a slow start into a guaranteed failure. The wait is now a retry, and the give-up point moved to nine seconds.

## A note if this keeps happening

This is a Bluetooth limitation rather than something Mindflow can fully remove — the audio route genuinely isn't up yet. A wired or built-in microphone avoids it entirely, and also keeps your headphones in high-quality playback mode.

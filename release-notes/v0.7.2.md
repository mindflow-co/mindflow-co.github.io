# Mindflow v0.7.2

**Released:** 2026-07-10

Mindflow now asks before it records, only reacts to real meetings, and comes with a free 30-day trial — everything still 100% on your Mac.

## What's new
- **Ask before recording.** When Mindflow detects a meeting, it now shows a small floating prompt — "Zoom meeting detected — Start recording?" — instead of silently starting a recording. Recording never begins without your confirmation. You'll find the renamed setting under Settings → General: "Ask to record when a meeting starts."
- **Detection that means it.** Mindflow now only considers something a meeting when a meeting app is actively using your microphone — not when the app merely sits open in your Dock. Having Chrome, Slack, or Discord running no longer counts as a "meeting". Browser calls are labeled by the browser ("Chrome"), since Mindflow can't tell which site is calling.
- **Free 30-day trial and offline licenses.** Mindflow is preparing for its commercial launch: new installs get a full-featured 30-day trial, and purchased license keys activate entirely offline — validation happens on your Mac with cryptographic signatures, so the app never phones home. Your recordings, transcripts, and privacy guarantees are unchanged.
- **Speaker identification progress on the meeting screen.** After a meeting ends, the meeting window now shows an "Identifying speakers…" banner while Mindflow processes the audio, and the transcript refreshes automatically with speaker names the moment it finishes — no more guessing whether processing is still running.

## Fixes
- Fixed a bug where, with auto-record enabled, a recording could start on its own whenever a browser or chat app launched — even though no meeting was happening. This is the bug the new ask-first prompt and stricter detection eliminate for good.

## Under the hood
- Recording tools exposed to AI assistants (through MCP) now respect the trial/license state.
- The microphone-activity check behind the new detection reads only system metadata — it never captures audio and needs no additional permissions.
